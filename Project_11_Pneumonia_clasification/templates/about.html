About PneumoScan

What is Pneumonia?

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs. The air sacs may fill with fluid or pus, causing cough with phlegm or pus, fever, chills, and difficulty breathing.

How Our System Works

PneumoScan uses a deep learning model trained on thousands of chest X-ray images to detect signs of pneumonia. The model analyzes patterns in the X-ray that might indicate infection.
